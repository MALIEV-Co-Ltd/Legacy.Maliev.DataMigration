Report hashes are references to executed evidence, never success markers. The reader requires authentic signatures, exact source and images, actual passed TRX identities, invocation and resource ownership, complete before/after fleets and catalog brackets, installed policy, scenario facts, recovery artifacts and verified cleanup.

## Separate authority modes

`PersistentWithClosedConsoleProof` requires no component subject and all four genuine completed closed-report references. Every existing persistent readback remains mandatory.

`DisposableNativeBootstrap` requires an authenticated component subject and explicitly empty future helper/native/delete/recovery fields. Nulls, zero-hash placeholders, borrowed reports and mixed forms reject. The seed binds the full three-key pin bundle, accepted source, owner closure, precursor, parent, current images and operation. The subject binds both actual disposable targets, complete inventories, preimages, policies, device proof and exact delete operands.

Claims authentication is separate from observation. A caller-supplied subject is not an independent source, image, precursor or target readback. The pure observed-context validator cannot itself produce those facts. Until the concrete held observer is implemented and tested, permit admission rejects first-provider bootstrap before environment contact. The public Console also rejects that lane. No interface, synthetic fixture or valid signature removes this fence.

A later closed disposable transition must consume completed genuine provider reports through a separately reviewed contract. It must not relabel a first-provider seed as a completed provider or closed report.
